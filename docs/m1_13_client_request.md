# Evidence request (for the client / data provider)

Our monitoring review of your distribution transformer is descriptive only: we
show readings, coverage, and gaps, and a person reviews every case. Before the
PoC can interpret readings or test any early-warning method, we need your help
with the following — in priority order:

1. **Meter units.** A short statement, per measurement name, of what unit each
   meter reports in (and any scaling applied). Without this, readings are just numbers.
2. **Clock information.** What timezone the timestamps use and how often meters
   report. Without this, trends cannot be trusted.
3. **Asset confirmation.** Written confirmation of which transformer this data
   comes from and that it is oil-immersed, plus its rating if available.
4. **Applicable rules.** Which maintenance rules you approve for this equipment
   and data, with the standard name and clause for each threshold.
5. **Past faults and repairs.** Dated records of confirmed faults or maintenance
   for these assets so future methods can be tested honestly.

How to respond: send documents, exports, or written statements with their source
and date; flag anything uncertain rather than guessing. Nothing here asks for
personal data. No request has been sent yet — this letter is a draft awaiting
your approval to send. No engineering claim in the PoC changes until a qualified
reviewer examines your evidence and records approval.
